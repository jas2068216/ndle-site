NDLE is an AI

NDLE is an artificial intelligence (AI) helper. When you talk with NDLE, you are talking with a computer program, not a person. NDLE uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. If you turn on Premium voice, NDLE's voice is made by text-to-speech companies (Speechify and ElevenLabs). AI can be wrong. Check anything important, like dates, money or names, before you act on it.

Not medical or other advice

NDLE is a personal scheduling tool provided as-is during private testing. It is not a doctor, therapist, counselor, lawyer or financial adviser. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or call 911 if you are in danger right now. See how NDLE handles a crisis.

18 and over

You must be 18 or older to use NDLE. By using NDLE, you confirm you are 18 or older. AI companion features may not be suitable for some minors.

NDLE never sends email

If you switch Gmail on, NDLE can read your mail, write reply drafts, and tidy your inbox. NDLE never sends email for you. You read every draft and press send yourself in your own mail app. Tidying means mail that looks like spam goes to Trash and receipts and newsletters are archived; nothing is ever deleted forever. By default NDLE tidies first and then tells you what it moved, and "undo" puts it all back for 24 hours. Choose "Ask me first" in Settings and every move becomes a question. An email that cancels or moves one of your plans is always asked about first.

You are in charge

You keep ownership of everything you capture. You may stop using NDLE at any time; "Delete my account" in Settings, "Start fresh", and disconnecting Google remove your data as described in the privacy policy.

NDLE suggests, and acts only in small, reversible ways. Anything that touches your contacts or another person asks you first. Inbox tidying (Trash, archive) acts first and tells you, with undo, unless you choose "Ask me first". NDLE may move flexible things on your calendar overnight inside the limits you set (work hours, quiet hours). It never moves a fixed appointment on its own. When you let a quest go, its calendar event is kept as a "(let go)" record rather than deleted, and NDLE clears only its own let-go records after the window you choose in Settings (30 days unless you change it). You can turn the overnight tidy-up off in Settings.

Your Google data

How NDLE uses Google data is in the privacy policy. You can remove NDLE's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
