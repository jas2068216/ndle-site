How NDLE handles a crisis

Last updated October 20, 2026

NDLE is an AI helper for planning your day. It is not a person, a therapist, or a crisis service.

If you are thinking about suicide or hurting yourself, or you are in danger:

  • Call or text 988 (the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US). Free, any time.
  • Or chat at 988lifeline.org.
  • Call 911 if you are in danger right now.
  • Outside the US, call your local emergency number.

What NDLE is built to do

NDLE's AI is instructed that if you talk about wanting to die, hurting yourself, or being in danger, it should:

  1. Stop planning. No tasks, no choices, no schedule.
  2. Answer with care, in short, plain words, and stay with you.
  3. Point you to 988 in the US, or 911 if you are in danger right now.
  4. Not joke, and not lecture or judge.

NDLE does not act like a therapist. It does not diagnose, treat or counsel.

No tool is perfect. NDLE may miss something, or may say something that does not fit the moment. If you need help, please reach out to 988 or 911 directly.

You are talking with an AI

NDLE uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. NDLE's voice is made by text-to-speech software. No human is reading or answering in real time.

Adults only

NDLE is for adults 18 and over. AI companion features may not be suitable for some minors.

Report a concern

If NDLE said something harmful, or did not respond well in a hard moment, please tell us. Email jason@ndle.org with the subject "Safety concern". We read every report.
