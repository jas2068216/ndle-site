NDLE

A talking second brain. No judgment, just handled.

Say it "noodle." You talk, it listens, and the thing you said lands on your calendar or your list. Nothing is ever marked late, overdue, or missed. Rest counts.

You say it

"Remind me at 2 to call the bank." "Brain drop" and talk as long as you like. NDLE catches it all.

It handles it

Your day, your reminders, your inbox. It tidies, then tells you what it did. Say "undo" to change any of it.

No shame, ever

No streaks. No red badges. A hard day gets lighter, not louder. "Today is too much" is a real command.

Your info is yours

NDLE connects to your Google Calendar, Tasks and Gmail only when you turn each one on, and you can turn any of them off any time. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to sell you anything. The whole story is in our privacy page.

For adults 18+. NDLE is an AI helper for planning your day. It is not a doctor, a therapist, or a crisis line.

In a crisis in the US, call or text 988. In an emergency, call 911. How NDLE handles a crisis.
